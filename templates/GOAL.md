# Goal: {{PROJECT}}

Written by Luis (via /grill-me with the orchestrator). Agents read this before
every decision and never edit it. Keep it under a page.

**Objective:** one sentence. What exists or is known when this is done.

**Why it matters:** who uses the result, and for what.

**A win looks like:** concrete and checkable. Numbers if possible.

**Stop or pivot when:** the signals that mean "drop this" or "change approach".

**Budget:** model spend per day and in total, compute and disk limits, deadline.

**What Luis cares about, in order:** e.g. correctness > speed > cost.

**Out of bounds:** things never to do, even if they'd help.

**Defaults when unsure:** what to assume instead of asking.

**Open questions:** things Luis hasn't decided yet. Ask about these, not others.
